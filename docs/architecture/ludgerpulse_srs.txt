1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) documents the functional and non-functional requirements of Ludgerpulse, a cross-platform GST billing, accounting and inventory management application developed by Ludgerpulse Technologies Pvt. Ltd. The document has been prepared by the Ludgerpulse product team based on the application's feature set, pricing/subscription plans, and app-store listings. It is intended to serve as a reference for developers, QA engineers, product managers, and business analysts who need a structured understanding of the system's capabilities, either to maintain/extend the existing product or to onboard new team members.

1.2 Scope

Ludgerpulse is a "most simplified" offline-first accounting application designed for small and medium businesses (traders, retailers, wholesalers, manufacturers and service providers), with a particular focus on Indian GST compliance. The product spans four client surfaces that synchronize data with each other:

Android mobile application

iOS mobile application (iPhone/iPad)

Windows desktop application

Ludgerpulse Web (LPWeb) — browser-based companion for cloud-hosted companies

The system covers sales and purchase invoicing, GST compliance (invoicing, returns filing data, e-Way Bill, e-Invoicing), inventory and warehouse management, receipts/payments, orders/estimates/quotes, 35+ financial and inventory reports, payroll, multi-user role-based access, data sync across devices, e-commerce marketplace import, third-party accounting software import/export (Tally, Zoho, QuickBooks, Busy, Excel), cheque printing, an audit trail, and a customizable add-on reporting engine.

1.3 Intended Audience

Product managers and business analysts evaluating or specifying Ludgerpulse-like functionality

Software developers and QA teams implementing or testing the application

UX/UI designers responsible for the mobile, desktop and web clients

Technical writers producing user/help documentation

Prospective partners/resellers evaluating the Partner Program

2. Overall Description

2.1 Product Perspective

Ludgerpulse is a standalone, commercially licensed accounting product — not a component of a larger system. It is architected as an offline-first application: each client (Android, iOS, Windows) holds a local, encrypted copy of company data and is capable of functioning fully without an internet connection. When a subscription plan with sync is active, data is synchronized automatically across a user's registered devices via Ludgerpulse's cloud service; local-only companies can alternatively be synced manually through Dropbox. A browser-based counterpart, LPWeb, provides access to cloud-hosted companies from any device with a browser.

The product positions itself as an alternative to traditional desktop accounting software (e.g., Tally) and other mobile billing apps (e.g., Vyapar, Busy, myBillBook), differentiating on ease-of-use for non-accountants, offline operation, and cross-platform licensing.

2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints

GST-specific features (e-Way Bill, e-Invoicing, GSTR filing formats) are applicable to Indian businesses only

Desktop licensing is capped per-plan (e.g., a Silver plan may license 1–5 desktop devices); mobile device sync allowances are governed by the same per-plan device count

Feature availability (invoice templates, eWay Bill/eInvoice quotas, number of LPWeb companies, payment gateway integrations) varies by subscription tier: Silver, Gold, Diamond

No macOS-native client exists at present (per user feedback on the Apple App Store); Windows is the only native desktop OS supported

Custom/add-on reports require the user to have working knowledge of databases and XML

The vendor states that customer-specific customization outside standard configuration is not offered — only general bug fixes and minor enhancements are released as free updates

2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies

Users have basic literacy in their operating business domain but are assumed to have no formal accounting training ("designed for non-accountants")

An internet connection is required only for licensing/activation, automatic multi-device sync, e-Way Bill/e-Invoice generation, and e-commerce import; core voucher entry works fully offline

Where GST/e-Way Bill/e-Invoice features are used, the business is assumed to be registered under India's GST regime and have valid GSTIN/portal credentials

Payment gateway features (Razorpay, Paytm, Cashfree, UPI, Stripe) assume the business already holds active merchant accounts with those providers

4. External Interface Requirements

4.1 User Interfaces

Mobile UI (Android/iOS): touch-optimized forms for voucher entry, camera-based barcode/receipt capture, and native share sheets for invoice distribution.

Desktop UI (Windows): full-featured data-entry screens, barcode-scanner input, cheque-printing dialogs, and the add-on report designer.

Web UI (LPWeb): browser-based access to cloud-hosted company data with a subset of desktop functionality.

All UIs shall avoid accounting jargon in favor of plain-language labels, consistent with the product's "no accounting knowledge required" design principle.

Barcode scanners connected to the Windows client via USB/Bluetooth.

Device camera (mobile) used as a barcode scanner and for capturing receipt photographs.

5. Non-Functional Requirements

5.1 Performance

The system should comfortably support the vendor's recommended daily voucher volumes per plan (≈100/day Silver, ≈300/day Gold, ≈500/day Diamond) without noticeable UI lag.

The system should support the vendor's recommended account/item master sizes per plan (≈5,000 Silver, ≈7,500 Gold, ≈10,000 Diamond) while maintaining acceptable search and report-generation times.

Report generation (e.g., Balance Sheet, GSTR reports) should complete within a few seconds for data volumes within the recommended plan limits.

5.2 Availability & Reliability

Core voucher entry and reporting must remain fully available while the device is offline; only sync, licensing, e-Way Bill/e-Invoice, and e-commerce import require connectivity.

Deleted vouchers must be recoverable (soft-delete/restore) rather than being immediately purged.

5.3 Security

Company data shall be encrypted at rest and protected by a two-tier password scheme (subscription password + company password).

Role-based access control shall restrict each user to only the modules and actions required for their function.

An immutable/append-only audit trail shall record all voucher-level edits for compliance with the Indian Ministry of Corporate Affairs audit-trail mandate (effective FY starting 1 Apr 2022).

5.4 Usability

The application shall require no formal accounting training to operate ("Designed for non-accountants").

Setup shall follow a "download, install, and use" flow with no mandatory onboarding training.

The UI/UX and feature set shall remain consistent across Android, iOS and Windows so that data and user familiarity transfer seamlessly between platforms.

5.5 Portability & Compatibility

The application shall be available natively on Android, iOS and Windows, with a browser-based option (LPWeb) for platform-independent access.

Data created on one supported platform shall be usable, without conversion, on any other supported platform for the same company.

5.6 Maintainability & Supportability

Minor enhancements and bug fixes shall be delivered as free updates to all active subscribers.

Users shall be proactively notified in-app when a new version is available for download.

Support shall be reachable via phone/WhatsApp (+91-99991 76746) and email (support@ludgerpulseapp.net).

5.7 Localization

The marketing/support site supports multiple Indian languages (English, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Malayalam, Odia, Gujarati, Punjabi, Assamese, Urdu), indicating a requirement for broad language accessibility for the Indian market.

Multi-currency invoicing indicates a requirement to support international/non-INR businesses in addition to GST-specific India functionality.

6. Data Requirements & Licensing Model

6.1 Data Requirements

Master data: Companies, Users/Roles, Customers, Suppliers, Inventory Items, Warehouses, Employees, Chart of Accounts.

6.3 Commercial & Support Policies (as published)

No refunds are processed except in cases of accidental/duplicate purchase, and only within 24 hours of purchase.

Purchases made via iTunes, Play Store, Amazon, Flipkart or a distributor must be handled through that channel's own billing/refund process.

Account activation is typically immediate but may take up to 24 working hours.

The vendor does not provide business-specific customization; only general bug fixes and minor enhancements are released free of charge.

A Partner Program exists for resellers/consultants who wish to onboard and support their own customers on Ludgerpulse.

7. Other Requirements, Assumptions & Appendices

7.1 Regulatory Requirements

Compliance with Indian GST invoicing, return-filing data formats (GSTR-1/2/2A/3B/4/9), e-Way Bill and e-Invoicing (IRN) rules.

Compliance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs audit-trail requirement applicable to accounting software used by companies, effective for financial years starting on/after 1 April 2022.

7.2 Assumptions

Feature-level requirements in this SRS are derived from the application's published marketing, pricing and FAQ content as well as internal product decisions; detailed internal design documents and source code are maintained separately by the engineering team.

Exact data models, APIs, and internal architecture are not publicly documented and are therefore inferred at the level of user-visible functionality only.

Feature figures (e.g., "35+ reports", quota numbers) reflect the product's published claims at the time of writing and may change with product updates.
